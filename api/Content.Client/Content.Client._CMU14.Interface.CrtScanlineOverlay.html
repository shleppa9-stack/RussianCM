Class CrtScanlineOverlay

Scanlines drawn straight over whatever is beneath, with no render target involved.

Remarks

The cheap half of CrtScreenControl, for surfaces that want the raster but must not be captured. Scanlines only darken pixels, so unlike the roll bar they need no copy of the source to sample at an offset - and that is the whole point here. Capturing a live, scrolling, constantly-rebuilt subtree such as the chat means its clipping and scroll offsets have to survive a re-entrant render pass, and a stale or misaligned copy is drawn opaque over the real thing, so it fails by showing the player old text rather than by throwing.

Nothing here animates. The shader drifts its phase with TIME; over a block of text being read that is motion for its own sake, and the lines are indistinguishable standing still.
